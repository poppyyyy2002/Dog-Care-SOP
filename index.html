🐾 Dog Care SOP

made by Poppy H.
Welcome to the Dog Care Manual! We hope this helps first-time dog owners get started quickly. Wishing you and your dog a wonderful time together!

Checklist

🍴 Diet & Nutrition Puppy/Adult kibble, stainless steel or ceramic food and water bowls. Please do not use gravity ball-style water bottles; they are unsuitable for dogs and prevent them from drinking enough water.
😴 Sleep & Security A comfortable bed or a pet-specific breathable cot. For dogs lacking security, use a playpen initially or buy a roofed kennel to help them feel safe.
🧼 Hygiene Essentials Pet pee pads, poop bags, dog shampoo, nail clippers, and grooming scissors/clippers. If you are inexperienced, it is recommended to visit a professional groomer.
🏥 Medical Readiness Phone number and address of the nearest 24HR veterinary clinic, and a sturdy pet carrier.

Exercise & Rest Guide

Dog Type Suggested Daily Exercise Exercise Tips Total Daily Sleep Rest Focus
High Energy
(e.g., Border Collie, Husky)		 1.5 - 2+ Hours Jogging, fetch, advanced scent work, agility training. 12 - 14 Hours Needs high-quality deep sleep; provide a quiet space to wind down after exercise.
Medium/Low Energy
(e.g., Poodle, Mixed breeds)		 45 - 60 Mins Leisurely walks, indoor tug-of-war, social interaction. 12 - 14 Hours Accustomed to segmented light sleep; a quiet environment is sufficient.
Small/Brachycephalic
(e.g., Pug, Chihuahua)		 20 - 30 Mins Scent games, short walks (avoid high temperatures). 14 - 16 Hours Quick energy depletion; needs frequent afternoon naps.
Puppy
(Under 1 year)		 Small bursts
(Age in months × 5 mins)		 Low-impact play, socialization exploration, basic commands. 18 - 20 Hours Critical growth period; lack of sleep affects development and emotional stability.
Senior
(7+ years)		 30 - 45 Mins
(Based on health)		 Slow walks, gentle massage, environmental observation. 16 - 18 Hours Joints tire easily; rest areas need good support (e.g., orthopedic beds).
