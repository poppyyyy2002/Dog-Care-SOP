made by Poppy H.
Welcome to the Dog Care Manual! We hope this helps first-time dog owners get started quickly. Wishing you and your dog a wonderful time together!

Checklist

🍴 Diet & Nutrition Puppy/Adult kibble, stainless steel or ceramic food and water bowls. Please do not use gravity ball-style water bottles; they are unsuitable for dogs and prevent them from drinking enough water.
😴 Sleep & Security A comfortable bed or a pet-specific breathable cot. For dogs lacking security, use a playpen initially or buy a roofed kennel to help them feel safe.
🧼 Hygiene Essentials Pet pee pads, poop bags, dog shampoo, nail clippers, and grooming scissors/clippers. If you are inexperienced, it is recommended to visit a professional groomer.
🏥 Medical Readiness Phone number and address of the nearest 24HR veterinary clinic, and a sturdy pet carrier.
