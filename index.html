Checklist

🍴 Diet & Nutrition Puppy/Adult kibble, stainless steel or ceramic food and water bowls. Please do not use gravity ball-style water bottles.
😴 Sleep & Security A comfortable bed or a pet-specific breathable cot. For dogs lacking security, use a playpen or a roofed kennel.
🧼 Hygiene Essentials Pet pee pads, poop bags, dog shampoo, nail clippers, and grooming scissors.
🏥 Medical Readiness Phone number and address of the nearest 24HR veterinary clinic, and a sturdy pet carrier.

Training Tips

Sit

Hold treat over nose. Move it back over head. Praise!

Stay

Use a flat palm. Start with 2 seconds, then reward.

Common Q&A

Toxic Foods for Dogs?
Avoid Chocolate, Grapes, Onions, Garlic, and Xylitol. They are extremely dangerous!
How to stop puppy biting/nipping?
Redirect biting to appropriate chew toys. If they nip, let out a high-pitched "Ouch!" and stop playing.
How often should I bathe my dog?
Generally once every 2-4 weeks. Bathing too often can strip natural oils and cause skin irritation.
First vet visit check?
Discuss vaccination schedules, flea/tick prevention, and deworming.
